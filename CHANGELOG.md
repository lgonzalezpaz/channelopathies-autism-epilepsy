# Changelog
## v1.0.0 (2026-11-03)
Initial open-source release accompanying the submitted manuscript:
"Cell-type-specific biophysical modeling reveals modifier-dependent phenotypic divergence in sodium channelopathies: a computational framework for the autism-epilepsy spectrum".
Includes complete biophysical Hodgkin-Huxley simulation routines, stoichiometric normalization of Human Protein Atlas bulk and scRNA-seq datasets, 2D parametric bifurcation sweeps, epistatic modifier modeling of Gabra2 and Cacna1g in Dravet syndrome, Sobol sensitivity analysis, and publication-ready figure rendering.
